# Rizz Work

## 🚀 The Future of Professional Networking

**Rizz Work** is a revolutionary AI-powered platform that reinvents how professionals connect — think of it as **Tinder meets LinkedIn**, where cold messaging is no longer cold.

---

## ❄️ The Problem

- Cold outreach on platforms like LinkedIn feels awkward, generic, and often gets ignored.
- Recruiters waste time combing through irrelevant profiles.
- Candidates struggle to stand out or make a memorable first impression.

---

## 💡 The Solution

Rizz Work uses AI to craft **personalized, engaging, and “rizz-filled”** messages that break the ice and spark meaningful conversations — whether you're hiring or job hunting.

### 👤 For Job Seekers
- Swipe through companies and hiring managers.
- Our AI crafts a custom message based on your background and their profile.
- Get noticed without writing the same intro 50 times.

### 🧑‍💼 For HR/Recruiters
- Browse top-matched candidates curated by our AI-driven ATS engine.
- Swipe to connect and send instantly personalized messages.
- Attract talent who actually vibe with your company.

---

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. **Create a profile**  
   Share your experience, skills, and what you're looking for.

2. **Swipe through opportunities**  
   Discover professionals and companies via a fast, mobile-friendly interface.

3. **Let the AI do the talking**  
   Our GPT-based engine writes messages tailored to each connection.

4. **Make the connection**  
   Start a conversation that actually goes somewhere.

---

## 🔍 Core Features

### 1. 🧭 Swipe-Based Discovery (UX-first)
- **HR View**: Candidate cards with photo, title, summary, availability.
- **Candidate View**: Job cards with role, logo, team vibe.
- Swipe right to express interest, left to skip.

### 2. 🤖 AI-Powered Matching (ATS + LinkedIn Scraping)
- Define requirements: skills, years of experience, role type, tools, etc.
- Our AI ranks and surfaces candidates using resume parsing, LinkedIn data, GitHub, and more.

### 3. 💬 Automated Cold Messaging
- AI-generated intros referencing recent activity, mutual interests, or shared values.
- Adjustable tone: casual, professional, high-priority.
- Candidates also get help crafting messages to HR based on job details and culture.

### 4. 🔥 Bonus: “RizzScore”
A proprietary metric that evaluates:
- Match quality
- Messaging chemistry
- Responsiveness probability
- Activity status (e.g., job-seeking or passive)

---

## 🧠 AI Behind the Scenes

- **LLM-Powered Messaging**: GPT analyzes candidate/company data to generate context-rich messages.
- **Semantic Search**: Matches based on deep understanding of skills, experiences, and job specs.
- **Behavioral Learning**: Adapts recommendations based on swipe history and conversation outcomes.
